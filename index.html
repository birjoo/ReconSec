FAQ

Questions, answered

Will the scan affect or slow down my website?

No. The free Security Snapshot is automated and non-intrusive. It looks at what your website and servers expose to the internet, and only covers the systems you authorise.

What authorisation do you need?

A short written confirmation that you own the website, or are allowed to request a scan of it. The request email we send you already includes the wording; just fill in your details.

Is my report confidential?

Yes. Your report goes only to you. We never publish or share a client's findings. Our sample report has every identifying detail removed.

How long does it take?

Your free Security Snapshot arrives in your inbox within 3 business days. Paid engagements are scheduled once we've agreed the scope.

What happens after the free scan?

You get your report and a prioritised action plan. If you'd like help fixing the issues or want to go deeper with ISO 27001 readiness or security testing, we'll talk. There's no obligation.

Do you certify companies for ISO 27001?

No. Certification is issued by accredited certification bodies. We assess where you stand, close the gaps and get you ready for the certification audit.
